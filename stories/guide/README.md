# Wild Hearts Tales: story guide
Short vertical animal stories (45-60 s). Emotional, painterly, narrator only (the animals never speak).
Files: series_bible.md, characters.md, story_arcs.md, narration_style.md, music_notes.md, flow_workflow.md.
Tool: ../tools/assemble.py (see ../README.md). Inspiration is the feeling of the film Spirit: Stallion of the Cimarron (separation, longing, captivity, memory, return). Everything here is original: no copied characters, scenes, names, music or dialogue.

## Short format (new)
See short_format.md: 5 Flow clips (50 credits) + motion stills = one episode per day. Template: episodes/ep01/story_short.json.
Stills for episodes are not stored in this repo.
