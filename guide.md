# Wild Hearts Tales: story guide

## Series bible
Channel: Wild Hearts Tales. Tagline: Animal stories of freedom and home.
Promise: one complete emotional story per video, about an animal separated from where it belongs.
Format: vertical 1080x1920, 45-60 s, 8 scenes of about 6-7 s, 25-30 fps. English narration (Arabic version possible with an ar voice).
Spine of every story (5 beats): 1 Home (warm, moving) 2 Loss (storm, flood, fire, trap) 3 Longing and captivity (grey, slow) 4 Memory (gold flashback, soft vignette) 5 Return (open landscape, music rises, one quiet final line).
Rules: original animals and names only. No humans' faces. No violence on screen. No text in images. Hopeful ending. Last line short.
Look: hand-painted 2D, golden-hour palette, wide landscapes, grain. Night/loss scenes go blue; captivity goes dusty brown; memory goes golden and misty.
Title pattern: "<Name>: The Long Way Home" style, plus #shorts #animalstories.

## Characters
## Ember (episode 1)
Dun wild stallion, black mane and tail, small white star on the forehead. Brave, curious, stubborn. Home: the high plateau. Always looks back toward the mountains.
## The gray mare (Ember's mother, no name)
Soft gray coat, calm. Appears only in memory scenes, gold light.
## The herd
Mixed browns and bays, moves as one. Seen far away when lost.
Consistency tip for image prompts: always repeat the full look line (dun coat, black mane, white star on forehead) and the palette per scene type.
Next animals (original): Sable the lone wolf cub, Marigold the elephant calf, Tern the wandering sea turtle, Ash the fox kit.

## Story arcs
1. Ember: The Long Way Home (horse, storm, captivity, railroad work, memory, escape, reunion). Done as episode 1.
2. Sable and the Silver River (wolf cub swept away by a flood, learns to hunt alone, hears the howl of the pack).
3. Marigold Remembers (elephant calf taken from the herd by a drought, follows the memory of the water road).
4. Tern: One Beach (sea turtle hatchling carried far by a current, swims a whole ocean back).
5. Ash and the Lantern Hill (fox kit trapped in a barn, escapes at the first snow).
Pattern to vary: change the loss (storm, flood, drought, current, trap) and the place; keep the five beats.

## Narration style
Voice: calm, warm, slightly low male narrator (edge-tts en-US-GuyNeural, rate -10%). Alternates: en-GB-RyanNeural, en-US-AndrewNeural.
Sentences: 8-16 words, one image per sentence, concrete nouns (wind, rope, iron, meadow). Max 2 emotional adjectives per story. No jokes. Present facts, let the picture carry feeling.
Pacing: leave about 1 s of silence after each scene's line. Last line is the shortest.
Captions: 3-4 words per card, lower third, white with black outline.

## Music notes
Default (automatic): the assembler generates an original slow bed: minor pad (Am F C G) plus soft plucked arpeggio. Generated by code, so no license or attribution needed. Ducked under the narrator with a sidechain compressor, final loudness -16 LUFS.
Optional: set "music": "file.mp3" in story.json to use a track you are allowed to use. Safe free sources: YouTube Audio Library (check each track's attribution), Pixabay Music, Free Music Archive (check per-track license; CC BY needs attribution in the description), Kevin MacLeod / incompetech (CC BY 4.0, credit required). Never use film or commercial tracks (no Zimmer, no Bryan Adams).
Mood: gentle start, tension at loss, bare at captivity, warmest at memory, full at return.

## Short format: one episode per day on 50 free Flow credits

Source of the numbers: https://support.google.com/flow/answer/16526234
(50 free credits per day, Veo 3.1 Lite 4/6/8 s clip = 10 credits, so 5 clips per day.
No free video generation in peak hours, about 14:00-17:00 UTC = 16:00-19:00 Cairo.)

## Structure (35-45 s, 7 beats)
| # | Beat | Source | Seconds |
|---|------|--------|---------|
| 1 | Home, the herd running | still + local motion (pull-out, dust) | 5 |
| 2 | Storm, herd lost | FLOW clip 1 (4 s) | 6 |
| 3 | Captured, the rope | FLOW clip 2 (4 s) | 5 |
| 4 | Dream of home | still + local motion (pan, dust) | 6 |
| 5 | Breaks free, runs | FLOW clip 3 (4 s) | 5 |
| 6 | Hears the herd on the ridge | FLOW clip 4 (4 s) | 8 |
| 7 | Return, last frame | FLOW clip 5 (4 s) | 4 |
Total Flow cost: 5 clips x 10 credits = 50 credits. Narration is trimmed to about 8-10 words per beat, so each scene is the clip (4 s) plus a short hold.
Keep the money beats (storm, rope, escape, ridge, return) as Flow clips. Quiet beats (home, dream) are stills with motion.

## How to use
- Generate each clip from the matching scene still as the START FRAME (image-to-video) so Ember keeps his look.
- Name the clips 02.mp4, 04.mp4, 07.mp4, 08.mp4, 03.mp4 or edit "clip" names in story_short.json.
- Run: python3 tools/assemble.py episodes/ep01 --story story_short.json --out ep01_short.mp4
- A scene with no clip falls back to its "image" with local motion (tools/motion.py). Fields: image, motion (push|pull|pan_left|pan_right), particles (dust|embers|none).
- If Flow credits run out mid-episode, the missing clips are replaced by motion stills automatically, so the episode still finishes the same day.

## Making clips with Google Flow (option B, by hand)
Free: 50 Flow credits per day, reset starts at first generation, no rollover. Veo 3.1 Lite: 10 credits per 4/6/8 s clip (5 clips a day). Omni Flash up to 10 s: 1 credit/s at 360p, 3 credits/s at 720p. Peak hours about 2-5 PM UTC (4-7 PM Cairo): video may be unavailable without a subscription.
Steps: 1 write 8 prompts from the story (same character line in every prompt) 2 generate 1 clip per scene in vertical 9:16 3 download as 01.mp4, 02.mp4 ... 4 put them in episodes/epNN/ with story.json 5 run: python tools/assemble.py episodes/epNN
Tip: Flow clips have their own audio; the assembler drops it and uses narration and music.
